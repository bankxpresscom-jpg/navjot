01 THE WOMAN BEHIND THE BRAND

Beauty with purpose.

Dr. Navjot Kaur is a keynote, guest and motivational speaker, an Amazon bestselling author and an educationist who has spent her career turning knowledge into opportunity.

She serves as Director of Administration at Quest International School in India and as an advisory board member to educational institutions. She is Founder and Chairperson of the World Academic Achievers Forum, the Youth Diplomatic Conclave and The Global Women Achievers Circle across India and the UAE. With a PhD and an MBA in International Business, her research focuses on work-life balance and innovative teaching methods.

Imparting the right knowledge, and encouraging women to believe in their dreams and unleash their true potential.HER MISSION
