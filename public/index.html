02 About

An educator with a regal sense of purpose

Dr. Navjot Kaur is an educationist, Director of Administration at Quest International School and an advisory board member to educational institutions. A PhD and MBA in International Business, she is an Amazon bestselling author, a keynote speaker and a global education leader. As Founder and Chairperson of the World Academic Achievers Forum, the Youth Diplomatic Conclave and the Women Achievers Circle across India and the UAE, and as Mrs. India Planet 2022, she brings the same conviction to every stage: beauty with purpose.

Imparting the right knowledge, and encouraging women to believe in their dreams and unleash their true potential. Her mission
